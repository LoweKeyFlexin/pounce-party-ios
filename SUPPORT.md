# Pounce Party Support

A game for cats on iPhone and iPad: prey moves across the screen and runs from a paw.

## Getting started

1. Open the app. It starts straight away: the background appears and prey comes in from the edges.
2. Lay the device flat on the floor, screen up, and let your cat find it.
3. Keep the device plugged in. The app keeps going until you come back, so the screen stays on.

## How do I get to the menu?

Press and **hold the round Menu button in the top right corner for one second**. A tap does nothing, on purpose: a batting paw should not be able to interrupt play.

## What can I change in the menu?

- **Prey.** Tap one to switch to it. Turn on **Mix several** to pick more than one; the game then rotates between them.
- **Switch games every.** With Mix on, how long it stays on each one: 30 seconds, 1, 2 or 5 minutes. If your cat ignores the screen it moves on after half that time.
- **Favourites.** Pick the games your cat likes and tap **Save these as Favourites**. The Favourites tile brings them back with one tap.
- **All.** Every game, in rotation.
- **Sound.** On or off. Sounds play even when the device is on silent, because they are how the game calls a cat over.
- **Calm mode.** Slower prey, quieter sound, easier catches. For kittens, seniors and shy cats.
- **Two cats.** Each half of the screen gets its own prey.
- **Paw paint.** Every touch leaves a coloured paw print.

## Common questions

### My cat keeps leaving the app

Turn on **Guided Access** (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access). It locks the device to one app until you triple-click the side or home button.

### My cat is not interested

Cats differ. Try a different prey: many prefer the small scuttling ones (beetle, lizard, mouse) or the fluttering ones (moth, feather). Try a bigger screen. Try a quiet room, with the device flat on the floor.

### My cat seems upset by the sounds

A few cats, mostly older ones, dislike sharp, high-pitched sounds. Turn on **Calm mode**, which is quieter, or turn **Sound** off.

### Will claws scratch the screen?

Glass screens resist cat claws well, but a screen protector is a good idea if your cat plays with claws out.

### There is nothing to catch for real

True, and it matters to some cats. Finish a session by handing over a real treat or toy.

### It never stops by itself

That is by design: a cat that has wandered off should find it still going. Hold the Menu button and close the app when you are done.
