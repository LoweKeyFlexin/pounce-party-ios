# Privacy Policy

**Pounce Party**
Developer: Aaron Lowe
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pounce Party handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad.

## Summary

Pounce Party does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information. It makes no network connections at all. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

## What the app does

Pounce Party is a game for cats. It draws moving prey on the screen and reacts when the screen is touched. It plays short sound effects.

## What the app keeps on your device

- Your settings: which prey are picked, your Favourites, and the Sound, Calm mode, Two cats, Paw paint and switch-time choices.
- A count of how often each kind of prey has been caught, so the app can favour your cat's favourites.

This is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, log, transmit, or store any of the following:

- Your name, email, phone number, or any account details
- Your location
- Advertising identifiers
- Crash reports, diagnostics, or usage analytics
- Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or anything else on your device

There is no account to create and no sign-in anywhere in the app.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking services.

## Network use

The app makes no network requests. You can verify this by enabling Airplane Mode and using the app normally: every feature works unchanged.

## Children

The app contains no advertising and no purchases, and it collects no information from anyone. It is not directed at children.

## Your rights under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws

Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete. We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL. Substantive changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top.
